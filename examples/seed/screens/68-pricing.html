Good to know

Do I need to bring my own gear?

No. Rental shoes, harnesses and chalk are available at the desk. Members rent free; day-pass climbers pay $6.

Can I cancel my membership?

Monthly memberships cancel anytime with 7 days' notice. Annual plans are refundable within 14 days, then prorated for freezes.

Is there a youth or student rate?

Students and under-18s get $4 off any day pass and 15% off Monthly with valid ID.

Do I need belay certification?

For lead and top-rope walls, yes. Take our 45-minute belay test at the desk for $10, or climb bouldering and auto-belays without it.
